Precalculus: Functions and Graphs, a Complete Course with Interactive Labs

Precalculus is the stage where you stop treating an equation as something to solve and start seeing it as a function with a graph. Rush through it and calculus becomes hard for the wrong reasons: the limits and derivatives are fine, but the factoring, the domain or the shape of a logarithm are not. This chapter gathers the whole of precalculus into 16 pages in the order they build on each other, with formulas you can cite by ID, worked examples and interactive labs where you drag a value and the graph responds at once, on a laptop or a phone.

In 30 seconds
  • Every topic here is a family of functions with its own domain, range, intercepts, asymptotes and end behavior
  • \(y = a\,f\big(b(x - h)\big) + k\) is a parent graph shifted, stretched and reflected, and it works for every family from parabolas to sine waves
  • Exponentials and logarithms are inverses: \(b^y = x \iff y = \log_b x\), so their graphs mirror each other in \(y = x\)
  • Trigonometric functions are read from the unit circle: the point on the circle is \((\cos\theta, \sin\theta)\)
  • A sequence is a function on the natural numbers, a series is its sum, and statistics describes data with distributions

Core formulas used throughout

H1 Domain rules

\[ \frac{p}{q}:\ q \ne 0 \qquad \sqrt[2n]{u}:\ u \ge 0 \qquad \log_b u:\ u > 0 \]

Functions with none of these (polynomials, \(b^x\), \(\sin x\)) have domain \(\mathbb R\)

H2 Transformations

\[ y = a\,f\big(b(x - h)\big) + k:\quad (x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{x}{b} + h,\ a y + k\right) \]

h and b act horizontally (on x) · a and k act vertically (on y)

H3 Quadratic formula

\[ ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \ \Rightarrow\ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \]

H4 Exp ⇄ log

\[ b^{y} = x \iff y = \log_b x \qquad (b > 0,\ b \ne 1,\ x > 0) \]

H5 Unit circle

\[ P(\theta) = (\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta),\qquad \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 \]

H6 Arithmetic · geometric

\[ a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d,\quad a_n = a_1 r^{\,n-1},\quad S_\infty = \frac{a_1}{1 - r}\ (|r| < 1) \]

H7 Counting

\[ P(n, r) = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!},\qquad \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!} \]

H8 z-score

\[ z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma},\qquad P(X < x) = \Phi(z) \]
