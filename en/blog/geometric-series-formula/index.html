    Geometric Series: Formula, Examples and How to Find the Sum

    A geometric series adds numbers that are multiplied by the same factor each time, such as 2+6+18+542 + 6 + 18 + 542+6+18+54. There is one formula to remember and one condition to check when the sum goes on forever. This article covers the formula, where it comes from, and the examples that appear most often in exams.

    What is a geometric series?

    A geometric sequence is a sequence in which each term is the previous one multiplied by a constant rrr, for example 2,6,18,54,…2, 6, 18, 54, \dots2,6,18,54,… with r=3r = 3r=3. A geometric series is the sum of those terms, 2+6+18+54+⋯2 + 6 + 18 + 54 + \cdots2+6+18+54+⋯.

    To find rrr, divide any term by the one before it. If every pair gives the same value, the sequence is geometric. rrr can be negative (the signs alternate) or a fraction (the terms shrink).

    Sum of n terms, and where the formula comes from

    Sn=a1+a1r+a1r2+⋯+a1rn−1=a1 1−rn1−r,r≠1S_n = a_1 + a_1r + a_1r^2 + \cdots + a_1r^{n-1} = a_1\,\frac{1 - r^n}{1 - r},\quad r \ne 1Sn​=a1​+a1​r+a1​r2+⋯+a1​rn−1=a1​1−r1−rn​,r=1

    The derivation takes two lines: write SnS_nSn​, then multiply by rrr. You get the same terms shifted by one place.

    Sn=a1+a1r+⋯+a1rn−1rSn=a1+a1r+⋯+a1rn−1+a1rn\begin{aligned} S_n &= a_1 + a_1r + \cdots + a_1r^{n-1} \\ rS_n &= \phantom{a_1 + {}} a_1r + \cdots + a_1r^{n-1} + a_1r^n \end{aligned}Sn​rSn​​=a1​+a1​r+⋯+a1​rn−1=a1​+a1​r+⋯+a1​rn−1+a1​rn​

    Subtract: all the middle terms cancel, leaving Sn−rSn=a1−a1rnS_n - rS_n = a_1 - a_1r^nSn​−rSn​=a1​−a1​rn, which gives the formula above. When r>1r > 1r>1 you can write it as a1rn−1r−1a_1\dfrac{r^n - 1}{r - 1}a1​r−1rn−1​ instead (same value, fewer minus signs).

    Frequently asked questions

    When does an infinite geometric series converge?

    Only when ∣r∣<1|r| < 1∣r∣<1, and then its sum is a11−r\dfrac{a_1}{1 - r}1−ra1​​. If ∣r∣≥1|r| \ge 1∣r∣≥1 the terms do not approach zero, so the series diverges.

    Can I use the sum formula when r = 1?

    No, because the denominator 1−r=01 - r = 01−r=0. In that case every term equals a1a_1a1​, so the sum of n terms is simply na1n a_1na1​.

    What is the difference between a geometric sequence and a geometric series?

    A geometric sequence is the list of numbers a1,a1r,a1r2,…a_1, a_1r, a_1r^2, \dotsa1​,a1​r,a1​r2,…; a geometric series is their sum a1+a1r+a1r2+⋯a_1 + a_1r + a_1r^2 + \cdotsa1​+a1​r+a1​r2+⋯.

    Where are geometric series used in engineering?

    In the response of digital filters, the present value of projects such as rooftop solar, total distances in repeated reflections, and as the starting point of the power series 11−x=1+x+x2+⋯\frac{1}{1 - x} = 1 + x + x^2 + \cdots1−x1​=1+x+x2+⋯ used to build Taylor series.
