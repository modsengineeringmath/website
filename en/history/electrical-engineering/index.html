    History of Electrical Engineering: who worked out what, branch by branch

    Electrical engineering is the youngest of the major engineering disciplines, yet it changed daily life faster than any of them. This section traces when each branch took shape, who laid the groundwork, and where those ideas still sit in the textbooks students use today.

    Written by Ajarn ModUpdated 12 Nov 2026About 4 min read
    Electrical Engineering1. Electromagnetism2. Circuit theory3. Power systems4. Electronics5. Communications & control

    Overview: from a parlour curiosity to a profession

    For most of the eighteenth century electricity belonged to natural philosophers and travelling showmen. People paid to watch sparks jump from a Leyden jar. The turning point came in 1800, when Volta's pile gave experimenters a steady current for the first time. Within about thirty years Ørsted, Ampère, Ohm and Faraday had found almost every basic law we still teach.

    The profession followed the work. First came the telegraph, then electric lighting and motors. The Technical University of Darmstadt created the world's first chair of electrical engineering in 1882, the same year Edison's Pearl Street station began supplying customers in New York. The AIEE was founded in the United States in 1884 and merged with the radio engineers of the IRE to form the IEEE in 1963.

    A rough way to remember it: the nineteenth century was the century of fields and circuits, the first half of the twentieth belonged to power systems and vacuum tubes, and the second half to semiconductors, digital signals and control. The pages below take each branch in turn.

    Electricity in Thailand

    According to the Metropolitan Electricity Authority's own history, the first electric lights in Siam were switched on at the Chakri Maha Prasat Throne Hall in 1884 (B.E. 2427). The generator was bought in England by Chao Muen Waiworanat (Choem Saeng-Xuto). That was only two years after Pearl Street. Bangkok had electric trams from 1894, earlier than many European cities.

    The utility structure Thai engineers know today came after the Second World War: the Metropolitan Electricity Authority in 1958, the Provincial Electricity Authority in 1960, and EGAT in 1969, formed by merging the Yanhee Electricity Authority, the Lignite Authority and the North-East Electricity Authority. The 22 kV distribution and 400/230 V, 50 Hz supply that Thai power students calculate with every day grew out of those decisions.

    How to use these pages

    Read them alongside the lessons. When Kirchhoff's laws come up in a circuits class, it helps to know he published them as a 21-year-old student. When you meet the Nyquist criterion, picture the long-distance telephone amplifiers that kept breaking into oscillation. History gives each formula an owner and a problem it was meant to solve, and that sticks far longer than memorisation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who is the father of electrical engineering?

    There is no official title. The name most often given is Michael Faraday, whose discovery of electromagnetic induction underlies generators and transformers. On the theory side it is James Clerk Maxwell, and in power engineering Nikola Tesla and Charles Steinmetz are frequently named.

    When did electrical engineering become a university subject?

    The first professorship of electrical engineering was created at the Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany, in 1882. MIT and Cornell in the United States started electrical courses at about the same time.

    When did electricity arrive in Thailand?

    According to the Metropolitan Electricity Authority, the first electric lights were switched on at the Chakri Maha Prasat Throne Hall in Bangkok in 1884, arranged by Chao Muen Waiworanat (Choem Saeng-Xuto).

    How did the IEEE come about?

    The IEEE was formed in 1963 by merging the AIEE (founded 1884, power and telegraphy) with the IRE (founded 1912, radio and electronics).

ผลเฉลยเชิงตัวเลข: LU จาโคบี เกาส์-ไซเดล แนวข้อสอบเมทริกซ์ ดีเทอร์มิแนนต์ คราเมอร์ การประยุกต์พีชคณิตเชิงเส้นทางวิศวกรรม การแปลงลาปลาซ: ตารางและ ROC แผนภาพโพล-ซีโร่และ ROC คุณสมบัติการแปลงลาปลาซ
