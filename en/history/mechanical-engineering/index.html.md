## Overview: machines first, theory after

Mechanics is an old science. Archimedes wrote about the lever before the common era, and Newton set out the laws of motion in 1687. But mechanical engineering as a profession appeared when there were large numbers of machines to design, build and repair: the steam engines of the Industrial Revolution. Britain's Institution of Mechanical Engineers was founded in 1847 with George Stephenson as first president, and ASME in the United States in 1880.

A defining feature of the discipline is that machines often came before the theory. Watt improved the steam engine without knowing about entropy; the Wright brothers flew before lift theory was complete. When the theory caught up, it always revealed the machine's limits and pointed to a better next generation.

## Mechanical engineering and Thailand

Steam engines reached Siam in the reigns of Kings Mongkut and Chulalongkorn through rice mills and steamships; the steam rice mills along the Chao Phraya were the country's first industrial plants. Today Thailand is one of the region's largest centres for cars and parts, so Thai mechanical engineers work daily with CNC machine tools, robots, air conditioning and power plants, all of which have their history in the pages of this section.

## Frequently asked questions

Who is the father of mechanical engineering?
There is no official title. The names most often given are James Watt, who improved the steam engine, and George Stephenson, first president of Britain's Institution of Mechanical Engineers. Some accounts name Archimedes for founding mechanics.

When was the first institution of mechanical engineers founded?
The Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE) was founded in Birmingham in 1847, with George Stephenson as its first president.

How is mechanical engineering related to electrical engineering?
Electrical machines, power plants, turbines and motion control all need both. Feedback control itself began with the steam-engine governor, and mechatronics now combines the two disciplines.
